# Monetization Playbook - agent-orchestration-benchmark

This file is a commercial planning artifact, not a guarantee of revenue. Use it to make the repository legible as a sellable product, pilot, workshop, license, or proof-of-value package.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** B2B AI platform evaluation
- **Primary buyer:** AI platform leaders choosing LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, or internal orchestration.
- **Revenue wedge:** Apples-to-apples benchmark for correctness, cost, latency, and deterministic replay.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Framework selection audit** - pricing hypothesis: $3k-$8k audit
2. **Custom benchmark pack** - pricing hypothesis: $10k-$30k benchmark customization
3. **CI eval integration** - pricing hypothesis: $2k-$7k/month eval maintenance

## Acquisition Channels

- Technical blog comparing agent stacks
- OSS benchmark report
- AI platform Slack/community demos

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Mock benchmark runs without keys
- Reports render reproducibly
- CI passes

## Risk Boundaries

- Benchmarks are decision support
- Customer workflows need custom fixtures
- Avoid universal ranking claims

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Publish sample report
- **60 days:** Build buyer intake form
- **90 days:** Sell one framework selection sprint

## Metrics That Matter

- Fixture coverage
- Replay stability
- Decision cycle reduction

## Positioning Note

Lead with the specific operational pain, then show the runnable proof, then offer a narrow pilot. Do not pitch the repository as a vague app; pitch it as a concrete workflow with evidence, risk boundaries, and a next buying step.
